ePoc (Electronic Pocket Open Course) is a free and open-source micro-learning solution: short, fun courses that work offline, that anyone can create, share and take on their phone.

CREATE YOUR OWN ePocs
With ePoc Editor, free and open-source desktop software (Windows and macOS), design a course without any technical skills: text, videos, audio, images and interactive activities. Preview it, then export it as a single .epoc file.

SHARE AND IMPORT
Import an ePoc or a whole collection from a file, a link or a QR code. Teachers, trainers, non-profits, companies: share your content with your learners, no sign-up needed.

LEARN STEP BY STEP
• Chapters of a few minutes, at your own pace
• Interactive activities: multiple choice, drag and drop, card sorting, reordering…
• Points, badges and a certificate of achievement
• Offline access once an ePoc is downloaded

DISCOVER INRIA ePocs
The app also includes a collection of free ePocs on digital science designed by Inria researchers (in French): artificial intelligence, data, privacy, the environmental impact of smartphones, robotics…

RESPECTS YOUR PRIVACY
No account, no ads, no personal data collection.

COMFORTABLE TO READ
Dark mode, adjustable text size and line spacing, dyslexia-friendly font.

AN OPEN PROJECT
ePoc is developed by the Inria Learning Lab under a free software license. ePocs can also be exported as SCORM packages for learning management systems.

Learn more: https://epoc.inria.fr
